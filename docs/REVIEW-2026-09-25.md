# THE VAULT — the 2026-09-25 review, and what became of every finding

The owner asked for a review of the code, the database, the design, the AI and
maintainability — «لو بكرا راح يشوفه مبرمج محترف». This file is the ledger: how the
review was run, what each fix batch shipped and in which release, the status of
every one of the 211 confirmed findings, and what is still open. The release
notes of each version (`docs/RELEASE-NOTES.md`) carry the measurements.

## 1. Method

- **18 lenses**, each read by its own finders against the v395 tree: `maintain:app.js`, `maintain:modules`, `bugs:app-1`, `bugs:app-2`, `bugs:app-3`, `bugs:app-4`, `bugs:data`, `bugs:food-body`, `security:client`, `security:backend`, `database`, `design-system`, `i18n:msa`, `ai:accuracy`, `features:workouts`, `features:food`, `features:body-home-settings`, `performance`.
- **77 agents** in all. Every medium and high finding was re-read by an adversarial
  verifier whose default verdict was «refuted»; the verifier's note and a fix
  assessment travel with each finding. Low findings were recorded without a
  separate verification.
- **217 findings reached verification: 211 confirmed** (14 high · 84 medium · 113 low) **and 6 refuted** (below).
- The confirmed findings were split by area into nine batches (1, 2a, 2b, 3, 4, 5,
  6, 7, 8 — 108 findings) and **103 low findings no batch took** (§4).
- **The rule every batch worked under:** every code change ships with a check that
  FAILED first — on the previous release, or with the defect planted — and its
  failure text is quoted in the batch's commit and release notes; a refactor with
  no behaviour change is proved by the fingerprint net (0 differences) and the
  suites.

| refuted | the claim |
|---|---|
| `maintain:app.js#4` | The largest views keep growing past the measurements CLAUDE.md records, and renderHome mixes nine data domains |
| `database#2` | vault_data_history keeps 10 PUSHES, not 10 meaningful versions; the force-pushed-over copy is gone within one workout |
| `database#4` | pending/29 brings orphan ai_usage rows back on account deletion, and its 'self-clears' premise is false: nothing ever calls admin_prune_ai_usage |
| `database#6` | client_errors retention depends on the owner logging into the Console; a crash-looping release can fill the free tier |
| `design-system#8` | Rows on the pre-v395 pattern: three different row skins beside .fig-row |
| `ai:accuracy#3` | Chat and recipe requests to the Worker have no client-side timeout, and the Worker's per-attempt timeout is not deployed yet |
